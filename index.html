03 — Work

Selected work.

Flagship · Agentic AI

Multi-Agent Platform

Ayn (أين) — Personal Travel Planner

An AI-native travel platform that curates and describes destinations across Egypt, then builds dynamic itineraries around the constraints generic planners ignore — health conditions, budget and personal preferences.

Problem

Travel research is fragmented across blogs, maps and reviews. Generic itineraries rarely account for health needs or budget limits, so travelers rebuild the same planning work manually.

Architecture

CrewAI agents divide research, curation and itinerary synthesis into clear roles. Tavily grounds recommendations in real-time search, ChromaDB supplies retrieval context, and FastAPI exposes the workflow as a clean API.

Impact

Converts scattered research into one coherent, constraint-aware itinerary. The modular agent design makes it straightforward to add destinations, tools and planning rules without rewriting the system.

Customer Churn Prediction System
